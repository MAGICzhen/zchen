Research Interests

My research spans theoretical, computational, and systems neuroscience.

Computational Neuroscience

Developing computational models to understand neural activity, representation, and information processing.

Systems Neuroscience

Studying how interactions among neural circuits support behavior and cognition.

Dynamical Systems

Applying dynamical-systems methods to neural computation and population activity.

Working Memory

Investigating the mechanisms that maintain and manipulate information over short periods.

Cognition

Exploring the circuit and computational foundations of flexible cognitive behavior.

Neural Modeling

Building biologically informed models linking circuit mechanisms with cognitive functions.
